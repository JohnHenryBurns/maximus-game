CHOOSE YOUR DOOM

Computer: click to look around with the mouse. W/S walk, A/D slide sideways without turning, Z/X turn if you'd rather not use the mouse. E to open, click or Space to shoot or swing, Q to drop, 1–5 to pick a pocket, Esc to let go.
Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks, tap the buttons. Turn it sideways.
Slot 1 is the pistol — it kills spiders. Slot 2 is the knife — it cuts webs.
A shotgun and a machine gun are hidden in the chests. M turns the music off, F goes fullscreen.
