🌌 THE DIMENSION FORGE

design a whole world, and a portal that takes you there

Straight talk first. Bedrock Minecraft has three dimensions built into it — the Overworld, the Nether and the End — and nobody outside Mojang can add a fourth. So we do something sneakier: we build your world in a place nobody ever goes. High in the sky above your own world, or far below the deepest cave. Your ground, your sky colour, your creatures, your rules, and a glowing portal block that takes you there and brings you home. It feels like a new dimension — because it's a place that only exists because you made it.

  1. Tap the button — a file like the_fart_nebula.mcaddon downloads.
  2. Open it from Downloads — Minecraft imports it.
  3. Make or edit a world, then switch ON Behavior Packs AND Resource Packs.
  4. Join the world. 8 portal blocks land straight in your inventory.
  5. Place one on the ground and stand on top of it. Off you go.
  6. Your way home is the portal block sitting right next to where you land.
