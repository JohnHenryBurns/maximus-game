6FORGE IT

when this is ON, your creation drops straight into your inventory the first time you play — no hunting through menus
  1. Tap the button — a file like my_item.mcaddon downloads.
  2. Find it in your Downloads folder and open it (double-click).
  3. Minecraft opens and says "Successfully imported!"
  4. Make a world (or edit one) → Behavior Packs → activate your pack.
  5. In the game, open the creative menu — your item is THERE. Or type /give @s max:my_item
  6. HOLD it in your hand to feel any ✨ powers you typed.
  7. It's a BLOCK — place it down, then stand on it for its ✨ powers, or right-click it (tap it on a phone) for its 🖱️ action. If right-clicking doesn't do anything on your version of Minecraft, come and tell me — we'll try it a different way.
⚠️ This works with the Minecraft that phones, tablets, Xbox and the Microsoft Store use (called Bedrock). If your Minecraft is Java Edition it speaks a different language — ask Dad which one you have.
😤 it's not showing up! — try these, in order
  1. Both packs must be ON. Every addon here is really TWO packs — the stuff (what it does) and the looks (what it looks like). In your world settings check Behavior Packs AND Resource Packs. If only one is on, it won't work properly.
  2. Leave the world and come back in. Packs only switch on when a world loads.
  3. Just ask for it. Open chat and type /give @s max:my_item — if that works, your addon is fine and it's only the menu being fussy.
  4. If chat says "unknown item", the pack didn't load at all — Minecraft found something it didn't like and threw the whole block away. Set the trigger back to 🦶 STAND ON IT and re-forge: right-clicking uses an old system that newer Minecraft may have dropped, and one bad ingredient loses you the whole block.
  5. Search the creative menu. There's a search box — type your item's name. Custom things often sit right at the end of their section.
  6. Still nothing? Turn ON 📦 PUT IT IN MY INVENTORY above and re-forge. That hands it to you the moment you join, no menu needed.
